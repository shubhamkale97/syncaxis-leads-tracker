Activity monitoring notice. For security and audit purposes this system records the actions you take (leads and customers added, edited or deleted, notes, data exports and permission changes), together with your name, the date and time, your IP address and the approximate city it maps to. Only a Super Admin can view these records. They are used solely to secure the system and investigate incidents — never for performance measurement. Routine entries are kept for a limited period and then automatically deleted; security-relevant entries are kept longer. You may ask your Super Admin what is held about you, or request its deletion.
